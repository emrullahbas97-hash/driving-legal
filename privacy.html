Privacy Policy

Driving Testing — UK driving test preparation app
Effective date: 8 October 2026

Driving Testing ("the app", "we") helps learner drivers in the UK prepare for their practical test and look for earlier test dates through cancellations. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes. The short version: your data stays on your device, there is no account, and there is no analytics or advertising tracking of any kind.

No account, no sign-in

The app does not ask you to create an account or sign in. We do not collect your name, email address or any other identity information, and we do not operate servers that store your data.

Information stored on your device

Everything you enter in the app is stored locally on your device only:

  • Your test date, goal dates and the onboarding answers you choose.
  • Booking details you optionally enter (driving licence number, booking reference, test centre). These are kept on your device so you can copy them quickly when checking gov.uk. They are never sent to us or anyone else by the app.
  • Your progress: read guides, bookmarks, checklist ticks, road-sign quiz scores and your gov.uk check history.
  • Reminder preferences and your AI Coach chat history.

Deleting the app deletes this data. You can also clear it at any time in Settings → Reset progress.

AI Coach and Anthropic

When you use the AI Coach, the questions you type (together with the recent conversation, so the coach can follow context) are sent to Anthropic, the provider of the AI model, to generate a reply. No other personal data from the app — such as your booking details or test date — is included in these requests. If you never use the AI Coach, nothing is sent. Anthropic processes this data under its own privacy policy.

Notifications

Check reminders and test-day alerts are local notifications scheduled on your device with your permission. They are not sent through our servers, and you can turn them off in the app or in iOS Settings at any time.

Subscriptions and payments

Pro subscriptions are purchased through Apple's App Store (in-app purchase). Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details. The app only receives confirmation from Apple that a subscription is active so it can unlock Pro features. Manage, cancel or request refunds through your Apple ID account settings.

Links to other sites

The app contains buttons that open official gov.uk pages (for example, the DVSA change-your-test service) in your browser. Once you leave the app, those sites' own privacy policies apply.

No analytics or tracking

The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. We do not profile you, and we have nothing to sell or share — we simply never receive your data in the first place.

Children

The app is intended for learner drivers preparing for the UK driving test, which generally means users aged 17 and over. It is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
